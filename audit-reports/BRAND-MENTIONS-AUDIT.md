## 1. Executive Summary

Hotel Astoria Alba Iulia has a **solid local commercial footprint but a near-zero encyclopedic footprint**. The brand is well-cited by OTAs (Booking 9.0/10, HotelsCombined 9.1/10, KAYAK, momondo, Trivago, Kayak-meta), local Romanian directories (Infoturism, HeyRomania, PortalTurism, Weddingo, Locatii-evenimente), Romanian hospitality press (Ziarul Unirea, ProAlba, Alba24, Ziare.com, Urbea Mea, AmFostAcolo), and Florea Grup's own corporate site. Google Hotels / Google Maps aggregation shows ~1,275 reviews and the canonical NAP.

However, **the brand has no Wikipedia article, no Wikidata item, no OpenStreetMap POI, no Trustpilot profile, no TikTok, no X/Twitter, and no LinkedIn company page dedicated to the hotel** (only generic Florea Grup references). This is the dominant AI-citation risk: LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) heavily privilege Wikipedia/Wikidata as primary entity sources. When asked "what is Hotel Astoria Alba Iulia," an AI will either ignore the brand entirely or — worse — confabulate from generic Astoria entries (Oradea, Budapest-Danubius, St. Petersburg).

**Top risks:** (1) entity ambiguity with other Astoria-branded hotels; (2) room count inconsistency (20 vs 30) propagated across OTAs; (3) no Romanian-language Wikipedia presence means no GEO/SEO moat in `ro` AI search.

**Overall Brand Authority Score: 62 / 100**
